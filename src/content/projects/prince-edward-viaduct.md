## Scope

Grascan completed removal of deteriorated concrete and repair of the exterior faces of sidewalls, wingwalls, piers and arches on the Prince Edward Viaduct.

## Working over four live corridors

Work occurred over the **Don Valley Parkway**, the **Metrolinx rail corridor**, a **pedestrian pathway** and the **Don River**, with various hoarding and protection measures in place to keep each one safe and operational.
