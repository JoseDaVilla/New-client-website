## Scope

Grascan rehabilitated the Bathurst Street Bridge, south of Front Street over the Metrolinx rail corridor. Work included:

- Removal of existing TTC rails, ties and concrete infill
- Reconstruction of the north abutment ballast walls
- Repair of structural steel and new expansion joints
- A new reinforced concrete deck overlay
- Structural steel coating of the truss spans
- New asphalt paving on the approaches
