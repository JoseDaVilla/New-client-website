## Overview

Construction of a new pedestrian and cyclist bridge over the Metrolinx railway corridor, providing a convenient and more direct route from **Liberty Village to King Street West** between Atlantic Avenue and Strachan Avenue.

## The structure

The bridge features a **five-metre wide covered deck spanning 55 metres** over an active rail corridor.
