## Overview

This high-profile project included **three rail-to-rail grade separations** of railway diamond crossings, a storm water pumping station and retention pond, retaining walls and construction of three additional GO rail tracks.

## Bridge slides

Four massive concrete bridge structures were **slid into place over separate weekends**, minimizing disruption to rail operations.
