In 2021 Grascan reached **Platinum status** after seven years as one of Canada’s Best Managed Companies, with Deloitte certifying Grascan as a Platinum Member of the Best Managed program.

> When Grascan was first named one of Canada’s Best Managed Companies in 2014, owner Angelo Grassa credited the award to the work ethic, dedication and innovative mindset of Grascan’s employees.
