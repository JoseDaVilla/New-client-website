Grascan has been **COR™ (Certificate of Recognition) certified since 2015**, reflecting a reputation for adopting leading-edge safety practices.

During the COR audit process, overwhelming evidence of a strong safety culture was presented, with all personnel expressing commitment to continual improvement and senior management instilling strong values into the fabric of the operation.
